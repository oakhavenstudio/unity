Empowering Every Creator with High-Quality, Affordable Assets

Oakhaven Studio is a independent development brand offering a collection of high-quality 3D models for Unity. The studio's goal is to provide affordable assets that don’t compromise on quality—perfect for freelance developers and hobbyists looking to make their projects stand out.

Oakhaven Studio creates versatile 3D models designed to fit a wide range of game styles. Whether building a fantasy world or crafting a futuristic sci-fi universe, developers will find flexible, easy-to-use resources with no restrictive single theme.

By keeping costs accessible, Oakhaven Studio ensures great assets are within reach for creators at every level. Developers are encouraged to check back regularly for new releases and to reach out whenever support is needed.
